How to Play

🎯 Game Flow

  1. ❓ Everyone gets the same question
  2. ⏱️ Answer before the clock runs out. Once it does, answers lock
  3. 👑 The Game Master reads every answer aloud
  4. 🚫 Blank, bogus or duplicate answers are out
  5. 🔁 Next question, until one player is left

📶 Everyone's phone

  • 🔢 The Game Master creates a room and shares the code
  • ⚡ Everyone answers on their own phone at the same time
  • ⏱️ The Game Master sets the timer, 5 to 30 seconds

🏆 How to Win

  • 🥇 Last one standing wins
