Act-O-Matic

Actions speak louder than words and look much dumber.

🎯 Two Players Step Up
Game Master picks 2 players

🎲 Roll for Actions
Each player gets a secret action
(e.g., "Riding a rickshaw", "Brushing teeth", "Flying a kite")

🎭 Act It Out
Perform your secret action
No talking, no sounds!
Use gestures, movements, expressions
Other player watches

🤔 Guess!
After acting, you must guess:
"What action did I just do?"

🏆 Results
✅ Correct guess → You WIN!
❌ Wrong guess → Other player WINS!

🔁 Next Round
Pick 2 new players and repeat!
