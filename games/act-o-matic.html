Act-O-Matic

Actions speak louder than words and look much dumber.

Two Players Step Up
Game Master picks 2 players.

Roll for Action
Each pair gets a secret action (e.g., "Riding a rickshaw", "Brushing teeth").

Act It Out
Perform your secret action. No talking, no sounds! Use gestures only. Other player watches.

Guess!
After acting, you must guess: "What action did I just do?"

Results
Correct guess → You WIN!   Wrong guess → Other player WINS!

Next Round
Pick 2 new players and repeat!
