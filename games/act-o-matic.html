How to Play

🎯 Game Flow

  1. 👥 Two Players Step Up: the Game Master picks 2 players
  2. 🎲 Roll for Actions: each player gets a secret action
  3. 🎭 Act It Out: perform it with gestures and expressions only
  4. 🤔 Guess: after acting, guess "What action did I just do?"
  5. 🔁 Next Round: pick 2 new players and repeat

🤫 Acting Rules

  • 🚫 No talking, no sounds
  • 🙌 Use gestures, movements and expressions
  • 👀 The other player watches closely

🏆 Results

  • ✅ Correct guess: you WIN
  • ❌ Wrong guess: the other player WINS
