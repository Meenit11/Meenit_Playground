How to Play

🎯 Game Flow

  1. 📱 One phone, pass it around!
  2. 👁️ View Role — Everyone sees their secret role one by one
  3. 💬 Describe — Describe your word in 1-2 words (or fake it!)
  4. 🗳️ Vote — Eliminate ONE suspicious player
  5. 🎭 Reveal — See their role (word stays secret!)
  6. 🔁 Repeat — Keep going until someone wins!

👥 The Roles

  • 🕵️ Agents — You have the main word. Find the fakes!
  • 🎭 Spies — You have a similar word. Blend in!
  • ⚪ Mr. White — You have NO word. Listen and guess!

Agents & Spies both see "Agent / Spy" — you won't know which you are!

🏆 How to Win

  • 🕵️ Agents Win: Eliminate all Spies + all Mr. Whites
  • 🎭 Spies Win: Outnumber or equal Agents (after Mr. White dies)
  • ⚪ Mr. White Wins: Get eliminated → Guess the word correctly = WIN! Or Outnumber or equal Agents and Spies

💡 Strategy Tips

  • 🕵️ Agents/Spies: Be vague but believable!
  • ⚪ Mr. White: Listen carefully, guess smart!
  • 🎯 Everyone: Trust no one!
